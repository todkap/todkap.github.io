What I do

Hands-on architecture in the age of agents

Now

I lead architecture and innovation at NCR Voyix, and I still write code every week. In 2026 I ran agentic migrations of production systems - Node to Go and .NET to Go - and ended up somewhere between VP and Forward Deployment Engineer: 305 commits in five months.

My team runs agents in the parts of engineering that used to be pure judgment calls: reviewing pull requests against our explicit standards, auditing containers, planning migrations. The interesting problems are no longer about getting AI to write code. They're about decisions, verification, and governance. That's what I write about.
